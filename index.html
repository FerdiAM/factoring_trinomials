AC Method Quick Summary

1. When $a = 1$: $x^2 + bx + c$ Find $m, n$ where $m \cdot n = c$ and $m + n = b$.
Answer: (x + m)(x + n)
2. When $a \neq 1$: $ax^2 + bx + c$ 1. Find $m, n$ where $m \cdot n = a \cdot c$ and $m + n = b$.
2. Split middle term: $ax^2 + mx + nx + c$.
3. Factor by grouping terms in pairs!
3. GCF First: $k(ax^2 + bx + c)$ 1. Factor out GCF $k$ from all terms.
2. Apply AC method to remaining inner trinomial.
Answer: k(px + q)(rx + s)
